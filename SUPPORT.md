# Support

## Getting help

- **Documentation.** Start with [`README.md`](README.md) and the
  [documentation site](https://anulum.github.io/scpn-mif-core/). The
  [architecture overview](docs/architecture/index.md) describes what this
  repository owns and what it takes from its siblings; the
  [validation pages](docs/validation/) and the
  [formal property catalogue](docs/reference/formal_property_catalogue.md)
  say what each result is evidence of, and what it is not.
- **Bug reports.** [Open an issue](https://github.com/anulum/scpn-mif-core/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
  Include what you expected, what happened, the commit or version you used,
  and the smallest input that reproduces it.
- **Feature requests.** [Open an issue](https://github.com/anulum/scpn-mif-core/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **Questions and discussion.**
  [Discussions](https://github.com/anulum/scpn-mif-core/discussions).
- **Direct contact.** <protoscience@anulum.li>

Problems in the FRC solvers, the NMPC runtime or other upstream surfaces
belong to their owning repositories; [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) lists the
ownership split.

## Security

Do not report security issues in public. Use
[Security Advisories](https://github.com/anulum/scpn-mif-core/security/advisories/new)
or <protoscience@anulum.li>, and see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) and the
[threat model](docs/THREAT_MODEL.md).

SCPN-MIF-CORE is AGPL-3.0-or-later. If the AGPL's terms do not suit your
